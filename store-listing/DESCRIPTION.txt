ConsoleTurbo adds keyboard-driven review replies, one-click JSON export, and bulk regional pricing to the Google Play Console — all running locally in your browser.

⭐ QUICK REPLY & AUTO-TRANSLATE
Publish a reply instantly with a keyboard shortcut (Ctrl/Cmd + Enter by default), no mouse needed. Write in your own language — if the review was posted in a different one, your reply is auto-translated to match it before publishing, entirely on-device via Chrome's built-in AI. Turn it off anytime from Options if you'd rather always publish exactly what you typed.

⭐ CANNED REPLY TEMPLATES
Save your go-to responses once, then press a shortcut (Ctrl/Cmd + K by default) to pick one from a floating list while replying. Templates support placeholders for the reviewer's name, review date, and app name, filled in automatically before you publish.

⭐ REVIEW NAVIGATION
Jump to the next or previous review (Alt + Down/Up by default) or page through the whole list (Alt + Right/Left), no scrolling or clicking by hand. Landing on a review focuses its reply box, and any unpublished draft you leave behind is discarded instead of left stranded.

⭐ PARSE REVIEW TO JSON
Hold a modifier key (Alt by default) and click any review to copy its author, date, app, content, and avatar image as JSON to your clipboard — a toast confirms the copy and briefly highlights the review.

⭐ PPP REGIONAL PRICING
Stop hand-typing 150 prices. Open a price editor, enter one base price in the side panel, and it converts that price for every market at purchasing power parity, filling them all in one pass with live progress and a Stop button. Each row shows today's price next to the new one and the percent change, with a toggle to compare markets in your base currency, and you choose the rounding style (charm .99/.90, nice round numbers, or exact). Markets billed in USD or EUR rather than their own currency are converted correctly instead of getting a mismatched local figure. World Bank data for 200+ countries ships inside the extension, so nothing is fetched — and nothing is saved until you press Save in Play Console yourself.

⭐ CUSTOMIZATION & SYNC
Remap any keyboard shortcut or modifier key from Options, map app names to your own short slugs for the copied JSON, and bookmark any Play Console page for one-click access from the popup. Shortcuts, templates, slugs, and bookmarks all sync across your Chrome profiles automatically, no account needed.

⭐ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything runs locally against the page you're already on. No review content or reply text is ever sent anywhere, and translation happens fully on-device. The only thing stored is your own settings, in Chrome's built-in sync storage.
