⭐ QUICK REPLY
Press a keyboard shortcut (Ctrl/Cmd + Enter by default) while your cursor is in a reply box to publish it instantly, no mouse needed. Works with both plain and rich-text reply boxes, and recognizes the Publish button in English and Spanish.

⭐ AUTO-TRANSLATE REPLIES
Write your reply in your own language — if the review was posted in a different language, your reply is automatically translated to match it before publishing. Translation runs fully on-device using Chrome's built-in AI, so nothing is ever sent over the network. Turn it off anytime from Options if you'd rather always publish exactly what you typed.

⭐ PARSE REVIEW
Hold a modifier key (Alt by default) and click any review's text to copy its details as JSON to your clipboard: author, date, app, content, and avatar image. A toast confirms the copy and briefly highlights the review.

⭐ CANNED REPLY TEMPLATES
Save your go-to responses once, then press a keyboard shortcut (Ctrl/Cmd + K by default) while replying to pick one from a quick floating list. Templates can include the reviewer's name, the review date, and the app name as placeholders, automatically filled in before you publish.

⭐ REVIEW NAVIGATION
Jump to the next or previous review with a keyboard shortcut (Alt + Down/Up by default) — no more scrolling and clicking through the list by hand. The review you land on is focused and ready to reply to. Page through the whole review list the same way with Alt + Right/Left. Either way, any unpublished draft you're leaving behind is discarded automatically instead of being left stranded.

⭐ CUSTOM APP SLUGS
Map each app's name to your own short slug for use in the copied JSON, matching whatever naming scheme your team already uses.

⭐ FULLY CUSTOMIZABLE
Change any of the keyboard shortcuts and the parse-review modifier keys from the Options page. Changes save and apply instantly, no reload required.

⭐ SAVED SHORTCUTS
Bookmark any Play Console page from the popup — give it a name and it's one click away next time, from the popup, on any page.

⭐ SETTINGS SYNC ACROSS DEVICES
Your shortcuts, auto-translate toggle, app slugs, canned reply templates, and saved page bookmarks follow you to any other Chrome profile you're signed into, via Chrome's own sync — no account or setup needed beyond being signed into Chrome.

⭐ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything runs locally in your browser against the Play Console page you're already on. No review content or reply text is ever sent anywhere — translation uses Chrome's on-device models, not a cloud service. The extension only stores your own settings (shortcuts, app slugs, canned reply templates, saved page bookmarks), via Chrome's built-in sync storage.
